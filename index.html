P1Prototype pick

Electric ducted fan

An electric motor spins a fan inside a duct. A cluster of two or three per limb reaches 350 N.

Energy
Battery or cable
Endurance
About 1 min
Exhaust
Cool air

Drawback: batteries can barely lift themselves.

P2Free flight

Micro turbojet

A small jet engine compresses air, burns kerosene and expels hot gas. One engine per limb is enough.

Energy
Kerosene
Endurance
Minutes
Exhaust
Several hundred °C

Drawback: hot, loud, slow throttle, expensive.

P3Burst only

Cold-gas nozzle

Compressed air or nitrogen from a tank is released through a nozzle. Just a valve, with no moving parts at the limb.

Energy
Compressed gas
Endurance
Seconds
Exhaust
Cold gas

Drawback: a tank empties almost at once.

P4Hazardous fuel

Peroxide rocket

Hydrogen peroxide decomposes over a catalyst into steam and oxygen. The classic 1960s rocket belt design.

Energy
Peroxide
Endurance
20 to 30 s
Exhaust
Superheated steam

Drawback: concentrated peroxide is dangerous to handle.

P5Tethered

Water jet

A pump on the ground or water sends water up a hose to the nozzles. Proven and cheap.

Energy
Remote pump
Endurance
Pump fuel
Exhaust
Water

Drawback: not air, and a heavy hose ties you down.

[ RULED OUT ] Open propellers: efficient, but too large and too dangerous beside a hand or foot.
